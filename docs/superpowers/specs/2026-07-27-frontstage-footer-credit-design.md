# Frontstage Footer Credit Design

## Goal

Add a restrained design credit to the C&C Detailing footer that links visitors to Frontstage without competing with C&C’s brand or contact information.

## Approved design

- Remove the existing `Appointment only` footer note.
- Place `Designed by Frontstage.` in the bottom-right position of the legal row.
- Keep `Designed by` in the site’s normal Archivo body font.
- Render `Frontstage.` in the authentic Alike wordmark typeface with a heavier visual treatment.
- Make the complete phrase one link to `https://frontstagewebdesign.com`.
- Open the external link in a new tab with safe external-link attributes.
- Keep the copyright in the bottom-left position.
- Preserve the existing stacked mobile behavior and the desktop left/right alignment.
- Use a subtle hover/focus treatment consistent with the existing footer links.

## Verification

- Confirm `Appointment only` no longer appears.
- Confirm the complete credit has one accessible link name: `Designed by Frontstage.`
- Confirm the destination is the verified Frontstage production URL.
- Confirm the Alike font asset loads locally rather than depending on a third-party font request.
- Confirm the footer remains readable on mobile and desktop.
